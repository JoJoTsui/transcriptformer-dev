# Establish B3 feasibility before expanding full-cohort production

Status: accepted by the project owner on 2026-10-02.

The next ticket 05 milestone is a bounded feasibility and acceleration study.
The completed pilot yields 5,111/15,705 paired scores (32.54%), while exhaustive
current cohort scoring requires approximately 1.07 billion forwards per model
arm; retaining current per-attempt pacing alone implies 8.44 years. Further
full-cohort implementation must be guided by demonstrated computational and
score-support feasibility. The 500-pair/80% reporting gate is retained, ticket
05 remains open, and zebrafish work remains excluded pending collaborator data.

This supersedes ADR 0002’s assumption that genome-wide impact scoring is cheap;
the multispecies finetuning goal and approved score definition are unchanged.
Any change to the scientific estimand, cohort-selection policy, null or uncertainty
method requires a separately recorded owner decision. See the
[review and feasibility evidence](../agents/grill-with-docs-review-2026-10-02.md).

The owner also selected assessment of the frozen score, null and bootstrap rules
first. Prospective cohort support and method-preserving performance changes may
be studied; scientific protocol amendments remain separate decisions.
