# Ticket 05: online search for an observed B3 comparison — 2026-09-30

## Finding

No reusable observed comparison satisfying this project's approved B3 method was located in the primary sources inspected. This is a bounded search result, not proof that no unpublished or inaccessible artifact exists. Published TranscriptFormer demonstrations and Geneformer deletion results supply related evidence; they cannot substitute for scores generated from this project's checkpoint and frozen embryo corpus.

The exact project target is [matched-target gene-ID context impact](b3-deletion-score-decision-2026-09-30.md), with the [approved descriptive matched-bin null](b3-null-method-review-2026-09-30.md) and [full-universe paired comparison](b3-paired-comparison-decision-proposal-2026-09-30.md). B3 is the project's analysis name, rather than an upstream public benchmark name.

## Access and method limits

The [original bioRxiv full text](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.25.650731v1.full), its PDF, the Science full text/PDF and the [Science supplement](https://www.science.org/doi/suppl/10.1126/science.aec8514/suppl_file/science.aec8514_sm.pdf) could not be fetched through the web tool; Science returned HTTP 403. Search also surfaced third-party transcriptions describing TF–gene pointwise mutual information and panels, but those transcriptions were not accepted as primary method evidence. Consequently this note does not claim to have verified the final paper's precise PMI formula, final figure numbering or all supplemental datasets. The first-party overview, official model card, current repository inventory and archived inventory were accessible.

No model weights, large corpus, archive or accelerator workload was downloaded or run. Inspection used webpages, source inventories and existing local decision documents.

## What can close the gate

An observed B3 comparison is a measured output of this project, not a literature requirement that can be satisfied by locating an unrelated plot. Before producing it:

1. Validate and freeze the prepared post-QC corpus, phase membership, independent embryo membership, split and intended comparison family; bind them to source hashes.
2. Identify each model arm and verify candidate finetuning provenance. A base/pretrained arm can yield its own observed descriptive B3 result, but cannot demonstrate project finetuning improvement.
3. Run the approved producer on suitable compute and retain per-cell observations, unavailable reasons, expression/dropout metrics, full score tables and producer hashes.
4. Check ortholog eligibility, at least 500 paired finite scores and 80% availability of vocabulary-joined pairs for a reportable full-universe result. These are project-approved reporting rules, not thresholds inferred from the papers above.
5. Publish observed Spearman concordance, coverage TSV and rank plot. Use the approved 2,000 coordinated embryo bootstrap only if both species have at least five independent embryos in the phase; otherwise label the observed result descriptive. Insufficient matched-null support may prevent a reportable comparison even with many cells.

Online evidence strengthens context and can supply independent validation targets. It does not close ticket 05's requirement for the actual project result. This search makes no claim that the local finetuning data are sufficient; that requires the separate corpus and embryo-support audit.
