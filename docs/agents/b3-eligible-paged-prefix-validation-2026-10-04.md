cooperative and 950-second supervisor limit, 4 GiB process RSS, 4 GiB available
host RAM, 20 GiB free disk and 200 MiB combined numeric working bound.
The optional process-group observer was disabled: GNU RSS records are process
measurements, not aggregate process-tree memory proof. The largest recorded
accepted GNU peak is 790,816 KiB in prefix replay, below the process limit.

Complete public-return monotonic timers begin immediately before the unchanged
public call and end after it returns, including publication and source seals.
GNU child invocation adds helper startup/imports/admission and its own final
marker seal; their recorded durations differ and are retained separately.
`elapsed_before_final_seal_seconds` excludes final marker checks/publication.
Supervisor elapsed values can be last-heartbeat timestamps and are not complete
child wall time. H02's controller records 510.081243 s before its final seal and
483.617305 s summed supervised invocations. Neither is whole-method cost.
