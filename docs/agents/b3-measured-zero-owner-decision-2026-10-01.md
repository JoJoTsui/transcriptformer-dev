# B3 measured-zero method decision — 2026-10-01

**Status:** approved for prospective implementation. The owner answered
“approved, implement and record this change” to the concrete
[measured-zero amendment](b3-measured-zero-amendment-proposal-2026-09-30.md).
This records authorization to implement the amended method; it is not an
observed B3 result or scientific readiness approval.

## Frozen method boundary

Develop a **separately identified and versioned** computational context
method. Retain the accepted v1 positive-token matched-target score and raw
schema without changes. For the amended null, admit a peer effect of exactly
zero only when the gene is in the prepared measured universe, its raw count is
zero before clipping, normalization and tokenization, and its deletion leaves
the complete native model input identical. Certify source row, source hash,
cell/physical embryo/phase/split, input identity, eligible original targets,
checkpoint and method identities. Require at least one eligible native target.
Do not invent a native token position for the certified zero.

Keep positive focal cells and their embryo weights. A peer must have either a
valid native positive-token contrast or a certified measured-zero no-op in
**every** focal scored cell. Unmeasured genes, positive genes omitted by
truncation, positive tokens without matched downstream targets, and incomplete
source identity remain unavailable. Keep the approved bins, at least two
peers, positive sample variance and sample SD. Certified zeros change the
peer distribution and its target convention, so amended z-scores cannot be
presented as v1 or as automatically calibrated inference.

The [proposal](b3-measured-zero-amendment-proposal-2026-09-30.md) is the
normative detailed rule and implementation order. Certification serialization
and the native mask contract must be resolved in a separate v2 module before
any certificate is accepted. The first execution gate is a bounded structural
preflight on the frozen real cohort. Actual scoring follows only if potential
paired support can reach the approved **500-pair and 80%** reporting floors.
The independent **60% input mapping** and **5,000 genome-wide one-to-one
pair** eligibility floors remain unchanged.

## Acceptance boundary

Under the approved v1 rule, the full-cohort audit found zero potentially
supported human–mouse pairs out of 15,705; that result remains the v1 status.
The amended method has no completed certification, structural preflight,
finite model scores, null variance, bootstrap intervals or observed paired
comparison. [Ticket 05](../../.scratch/multispecies-readiness-remediation/issues/05-statistic-specific-ortholog-eligibility.md)
remains open. This decision does not authorize a GPU run, full training,
threshold reduction, biological knockout claim or zebrafish work.
