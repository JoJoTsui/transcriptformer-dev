## Resource and publication contract

Every action uses a fresh output directory. The inner cap is900 seconds,
supervisor cap950 seconds, process RSS4GiB, available host RAM floor4GiB and
free disk floor20GiB. Scientific/native numeric working arrays retain their
200MiB cap. CPU threads remain1 and CUDA is hidden. There is one heavy process
at a time. No stage raises a cap, removes a scientific gate or invents a
legacy pilot origin. The actual five-cell native importer remains within its
original48-cell interface.

The helper admits wall/RAM/disk/RSS before reading or compiling a dependency.
It executes the original study helper from the exact already-hash-verified
buffer and separately binds its own source and these instructions. Before
scientific imports and at final seal it validates the complete original frozen
script/native source membership and byte map. Adding a tracked script later
requires a new original study attempt; old receipts are preserved. File hashes
stream in chunks at most1MiB. Metadata reads have a32MiB limit and reject
duplicate JSON keys and nonfinite constants. Consumed references use strict
integer byte counts and canonical absolute paths.

A follow-on `summary.json` is valid only after marker fsync and final checks of
all consumed/generated/source bytes. Creation, fsync and these checks share
the failure boundary. A failed stage preserves its generated public outputs,
invalidates any top completion marker as `invalid-summary*.json`, writes and
fsyncs `failure.json`, and exits unsuccessfully. Consumers require the top
completed stage with the identical follow-on freeze. This is a cooperative
local-writer contract, not a claim of crash durability or protection against
arbitrary same-user mutations outside the before/after attestation boundary.

## Cost and artifact interpretation

The follow-on public-call timer starts immediately before the public function
and stops after it returns, including that function's publication and source
seals. Module imports and the study's own input admission/seal are additional
costs recorded by the external GNU time `-v` receipt. The helper's
`elapsed_before_final_seal_seconds` excludes its final marker/byte checks.
Supervisor `elapsed_seconds` can be a last heartbeat, so it must not stand in
for completed child wall cost. Every attempt keeps its separate GNU receipt,
supervisor state, stdout/stderr and failed/complete top-level study receipt.

`b3_eligible_paged_prefix_acceptance_freeze_v1`,
`b3_eligible_paged_prefix_acceptance_stage_v1`, and
`b3_eligible_paged_prefix_acceptance_cost_gate_v1`. They bind the original C
receipt files and this distinct helper rather than claiming original C or
original scientific producers emitted these study envelopes. Public nested
outputs keep their real producer schemas.
